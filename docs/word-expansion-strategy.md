## Strategy

### 1. Source Selection

**Primary Source**: SOWPODS (Scrabble Players Dictionary)
- ~280,000 words total
- Professionally curated
- Excludes proper nouns, abbreviations, offensive words

**Secondary Source**: Word frequency data (Google Ngrams, COCA corpus)
- Filters out obscure words
- Ensures players recognize the words

### 2. Filtering Criteria

Words to **INCLUDE**:
- Appears in SOWPODS or TWL dictionary
- Frequency rank in top 50,000 most common words
- No proper nouns (names, places, brands)
- No offensive/vulgar terms
- No highly technical jargon

Words to **EXCLUDE**:
- Scientific Latin names (QUERCUS, FIBULA)
- Archaic/obsolete words (FORSOOTH, PRITHEE)
- Regional dialects not widely known (BRAW, CLAG)
- Abbreviations and acronyms (ASAP, NATO)
- Slang that's too ephemeral (YEET - debatable)

### 3. Quality Tiers

**Tier 1 (Must Have)**: Common everyday words
- BEACH, CHAIR, DANCE, DREAM, EARTH, FRUIT, GLASS, HORSE, IMAGE, JUICE...

**Tier 2 (Should Have)**: Less common but recognizable
- BRISK, CLAMP, DWARF, EVADE, FLASK, GLEAM, HINGE, IVORY, KNEEL...

**Tier 3 (Nice to Have)**: Valid Scrabble words most would recognize
- ADORN, BLURT, CROAK, DOUSE, EJECT, FLECK, GLOAT, HASTE, INFER...

### 4. Expansion Process

#### Step 1: Automated Candidate Generation
```bash
# Pseudocode for finding candidates
1. Load full SOWPODS dictionary
2. Filter to target length (5 or 6 letters)
3. Exclude words already in our list
4. Rank by word frequency
5. Take top 1000 candidates
```

#### Step 2: Manual Review
- Review candidates in batches of 100
- Mark as: ACCEPT, REJECT, or REVIEW
- Focus on "would a casual player recognize this?"

#### Step 3: Testing
- Add new words to the game
- Playtest to see if they feel natural
- Remove any that cause confusion
